# Acme Corp HR Policies

## Leave Entitlement
Every full-time employee is entitled to the following leave per calendar year (January to December):
- Annual Leave: 18 working days
- Sick Leave: 10 working days
- Casual Leave: 6 working days
Leave is counted in working days only. Saturdays, Sundays and company holidays falling inside a leave period are not deducted.

## Annual Leave Rules
Annual leave requests longer than 2 working days must be applied for at least 7 calendar days before the start date. Annual leave must be approved by the reporting manager. A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the next calendar year; any remaining unused days lapse on 31 December.

## Probation Period
New employees are on probation for the first 6 months from their joining date. Employees on probation are not eligible for annual leave. During probation, employees may use sick leave and casual leave.

## Casual Leave Rules
Casual leave is meant for short personal matters. A single casual leave request cannot exceed 3 consecutive working days. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be encashed.

## Sick Leave Rules
Sick leave can be applied for on the same day by informing the manager. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive working days. Unused sick leave lapses at the end of the calendar year.

## Leave Without Balance
If an employee does not have enough leave balance, the excess days are treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP), subject to manager and HR approval.

## Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Employees on probation may work from home only 1 day per week.

## Company Holidays
The company observes a fixed list of public holidays each year. The holiday list is published by HR in January. Holidays are not counted as leave days.

## Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to treat colleagues with respect, protect confidential company information, and report any harassment or misconduct to HR at hr@acme.example. Violations may lead to disciplinary action.
